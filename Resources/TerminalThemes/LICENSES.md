# Terminal Theme Sources

The 20 terminal themes in this directory are original ServerDash palettes
created for this application. They do not include third-party iTerm2 theme
files or redistributed font binaries.

System fonts are discovered through AppKit and remain installed and licensed
by macOS or by the user. ServerDash does not copy or redistribute those fonts.

SwiftTerm retains its upstream MIT license in `Vendor/SwiftTerm/LICENSE`.
